# SentinelCore Advanced Agentic Investigation Platform

![ChatWindow](assets/ChatWindow.png)

## SentinelCore is a state of the art multi-agent investigation platform that learns from every case, every interaction to speed up future case resolutions. The system learns your environment and your needs and adapts.

## It can use your local models or premium cloud models, no special configuration or adapter needed.

## What sets SentinelCore apart is the pattern memory and the use of agent swarms to interrogate the target to solve the task.

## Division of responsibility between the agents allow the agents to reason over the information with being bogged down with too many constraints or trying to handle dozens of tasks at the same time.
